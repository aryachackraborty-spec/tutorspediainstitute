## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) Name Compliance Audit

### Issue Identified:
The screenshot of the primary Google Business Profile indicates a name formatted along the lines of:  
`Tutorspedia Institute - Best Coaching Centre in Dum Dum Park | CBSE | ICSE`

### Risk & Google Guidelines Analysis:
According to [Google's Guidelines for Representing Your Business on Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177):
> *"Your name should reflect your business’s real-world name, as used consistently on your storefront, website, stationery, and as recognized by customers. Adding unnecessary information, such as marketing taglines, city names, or keywords to your name is a violation of Google's policies and risks algorithmic suspension or manual penalty."*

### Recommendation for Institute Leadership:
1. **Gradual Transition to Compliant Name:**
   - **Recommended Compliant Name for Branch 1:** `Tutorspedia Institute` (or `Tutorspedia Institute - Dum Dum Park Branch` if distinguishing multi-location branches).
   - If the business registration or storefront signage officially includes "Dum Dum Park", it may be retained. However, marketing keywords like `"Best Coaching Centre | CBSE | ICSE"` should be removed from the profile title.
2. **Move Keywords to the Categories & Description:**
   - All high-intent keywords (CBSE, ICSE, coaching center, school tuition) belong in the **Services**, **Primary Category**, and **Business Description** fields where Google explicitly expects them and indexes them without suspension risk.

## 4. Ethical Reputation & Review Strategy

Reviews are a primary local pack ranking factor. Google strictly penalizes review gating, incentivized ratings, or fake testimonials.

### 4-Step Review Generation Protocol:
1. **Timing the Request:** Ask parents 3–4 weeks after enrollment or immediately following a successful unit test result or positive PTM.
2. **Direct Shortlink Sharing:** Create a direct Google Review shortlink from each profile dashboard (`https://g.page/r/.../review`) and send via WhatsApp:
   > *"Dear [Parent Name], we hope [Student Name] is enjoying their classes at Tutorspedia Institute [Dum Dum Park / Satgachi]. Your honest feedback helps us maintain our teaching standards and helps other parents find quality education. Would you take 60 seconds to share your experience on our Google page? [Link]"*
3. **Response Protocol for All Reviews:**
   - Respond to 100% of reviews within 48 hours.
   - Mention the subject, class, or program naturally in your reply (e.g., *"Thank you Mr. Roy! We are thrilled to see Rohan's improvement in ICSE Class 10 Mathematics. Best wishes from our faculty team!"*).
4. **Handling Critical Feedback:**
   - Respond professionally without being defensive. Offer an offline resolution phone call with the centre coordinator.
